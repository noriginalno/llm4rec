      Behind the recommendations

      Each movie is an 18-dimensional binary genre vector. Item-to-item compares candidates with your active movie. Profile-based first averages the original, unnormalized vectors of every movie on your shelf, then compares candidates with that average.

      Both lists use cosine similarity: the dot product divided by the two vector lengths. The score is a genre match, not a probability you’ll enjoy the movie. With one selection the lists are identical.

      All selected movies are excluded from both lists. Scores are rounded to 12 decimal places for tie-breaking, then sorted by ascending MovieLens ID. Only positive matches are displayed; missing genre information cannot produce a match. Rating counts show popularity and never affect ranking.

      This is a small historical catalog from 1997–1998, with coarse genre labels rather than plot, cast, or current releases. Your shelf stays in this tab and is not sent anywhere.
